## Before you start

After surgery, follow your surgeon's or physical therapist's protocol; it comes first. A hot, swollen joint, or one that gets stuck so you can't fully bend or straighten it, needs a clinician, not more exercise.

## How strong is the evidence?

The acceptable pain guide and the adjustment rules are clinical guidance that physical therapists use. They have not been tested as safety limits for arthritis, so they are graded *Unproven-Bridge*. The suggestion to cut back to about half after a day that does not settle is Randy Bauer, PT's clinical guidance, not a research-tested rule.
