# A5 blog cluster: image briefs (optional)

Each post already carries one inline HTML visual built from `mm.css` classes
(see `build-notes.md`). These briefs are optional header images for Randy to
make or approve. `image:` stays empty in every post until a real file exists
in `site/assets/blog/`; no image path has been invented.

Rules for any image: no text baked in that makes a health claim, no emoji, no
logos other than Muscle-Meta Matrix™, real-looking adults 50 and over, brand
colours only (teal, gold, ink on the warm off-white ground), JPG or WebP about
1600 px wide and under 300 KB.

| Post | What the image shows | Alt text |
|---|---|---|
| `why-rest-is-the-wrong-prescription-for-a-sore-joint` | An adult in their 60s seated on a kitchen chair, slowly straightening one knee, morning light. Calm, ordinary, at home. | Older adult sitting on a chair and gently straightening one knee |
| `the-5-out-of-10-rule-and-how-to-read-a-flare` | A simple 0 to 10 scale drawn as a line, with 0 to 5 shaded teal and 6 to 10 left plain, and a small note "next day: back to your usual?". Diagram, not a photo. | A 0 to 10 pain scale with 0 to 5 marked as the acceptable range during activity |
| `choosing-an-exercise-for-a-painful-knee-or-hip` | Four small panels: an indoor bike, a pool, a yoga mat, a resistance band. No people's faces needed. | Four exercise options for knee arthritis: cycling, water exercise, yoga and resistance training |
| `what-the-runner-data-does-and-does-not-show` | An adult in their 50s on an easy jog on a park path, relaxed pace, not racing. | Adult in their 50s jogging at an easy pace on a park path |

If an image is approved, set `image:` and `image_alt:` in the post's
frontmatter (the build refuses an image without alt text).
