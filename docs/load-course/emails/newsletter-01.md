## Body

Hello,

When a knee or hip hurts, the first instinct is to keep still and protect it. For a few days during a flare, that can help. As a long-term plan, it tends to work against the joint.

This week's article explains why. Cartilage has no blood supply of its own. It relies on movement to push joint fluid in and out, which is how it gets its nourishment. [CLAIM-C03] Long stretches of stillness take that away.

The article also looks at what large studies of runners show about joints and activity, and what they cannot show. Recreational runners had lower rates of knee and hip arthritis than inactive people in that research. That is an association, not proof that running protects a joint. [CLAIM-C01]

It ends with one small step: five minutes of gentle movement a day.

[Read the article](https://muscle-meta.com/blog/why-rest-is-the-wrong-prescription-for-a-sore-joint/)
