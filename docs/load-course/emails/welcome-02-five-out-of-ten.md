## Body

Hello,

When a knee or hip aches halfway through a walk, the natural move is to stop. The ache feels like a warning that something is wearing down.

Pain is your body's alarm system. With arthritis, a moderate ache during exercise does not by itself mean the joint is being damaged. Physical therapists often use a simple guideline to help people keep moving without guessing.

**Acceptable pain guide.** During activity, many clinicians treat an ache up to about 5 out of 10 as acceptable. By the next day it should be back to your usual. If it is not, do less next time. This is clinical guidance, not a tested safety limit. After surgery, follow your surgeon's or physical therapist's protocol. A hot, swollen joint, or one that gets stuck so you can't fully bend or straighten it, needs a clinician, not more exercise. This does not replace advice from a clinician who knows your history.

One harder day is information about the dose, not a sign you should stop moving.

The guideline is easier to use when you write it down. The free acceptable pain loading tracker gives you one line a day: what you did, how it felt, and whether the joint was back to its usual by the next day. There is no sign-up, and it stays on paper or on your own device.
