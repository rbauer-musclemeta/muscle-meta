## Body

Hello,

When people decide to take a sore joint seriously, they often start big: a long walk, a new class, a full routine on day one. Then the joint flares, and the flare feels like proof that exercise was a mistake.

More often, the plan asked too much at once and the joint had no time to adjust.

That is why the first step in Load as Medicine is small on purpose. Five minutes of gentle movement through a comfortable range. Sit in a chair and slowly straighten and bend your knees, or stand holding a counter and make slow, easy hip circles. The aim is to get the joint moving, not to test it.

A small start does two jobs. It gives the joint regular movement, which cartilage depends on for its nourishment. [CLAIM-C03] It also gives you a run of days where moving went fine, and that record is a better answer to worry than any reassurance from me.

If five minutes feels easy for a week, add a little. If it does not, stay at five. Either way you are learning how your own joint responds.

After a joint replacement, your surgeon's plan comes first.
