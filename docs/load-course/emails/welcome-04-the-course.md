## Body

Hello,

Over the past ten days you've had the guide, the acceptable pain guideline and the case for a small first step. Those ideas come from a short course I wrote, called Load as Medicine. It is an educational course, not treatment.

There are three lessons.

1. Why rest is the wrong prescription. How joint fluid reaches cartilage, why a short rest has its place, and how to get a stiff joint moving again.
2. The acceptable pain guideline. How to read an ache, and what to do when a joint flares.
3. Finding your exercise. In one analysis of trials in knee arthritis, all five types of exercise studied helped, including cycling, water exercise, yoga and resistance training. The lesson goes through those options and helps you choose one you will keep doing.

Each lesson has a video of about two minutes with me, a short interactive exercise, a tracker and a download.

The course is written for adults 50 and over living with knee or hip arthritis. That includes people working back after a knee replacement and people who want to keep playing their sport. It does not diagnose anything, and it does not replace care from your own clinician.

The price is $37, paid once.

The course page has the full outline, who the course suits and who it does not, and the research behind each lesson.

[Read about the course](https://muscle-meta.com/courses/load-course/)

If now is not the right time, nothing changes. The guide and the tracker are yours to keep.

Randy
