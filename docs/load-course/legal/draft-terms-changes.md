## 2. Reasons

- The current page is a placeholder that says it must be written before payment
  is enabled. `docs/legal-requirements.md` and the placeholder both note that
  Stripe requires published terms and a refund policy.
- PLAN.md D2: site-hosted, Stripe, $37.00 one-time (assumed). The price is
  therefore marked to confirm.
- PLAN.md D7, confirmed at G0: subtitle "An educational course, not treatment."
  The terms repeat it.
- `docs/matrixapp.md`, "Selling (M4, Stripe)": a one-off course carries a
  lifetime or fixed-term entitlement. The access period is a business decision,
  so it is marked to confirm.
- Lesson text and videos are paid content (CLAUDE.md 7.6). The no-transfer and
  no-resale terms back up the technical controls.
- Nothing is restricted by pillar or category. The terms talk only about a
  product (the course), in line with CLAUDE.md section 1.
- No outcome, guarantee or clinical benefit is promised. The text states that no
  particular result is promised.
