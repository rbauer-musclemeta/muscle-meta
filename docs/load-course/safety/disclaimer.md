# Medical disclaimer

The medical disclaimer for every page, download, email and lesson in the Load
as Medicine set. CLAUDE.md 7.5: each lesson, tool and download ends with it.

## Source and changes

The standing wording on `site/legal/medical-disclaimer/index.html` is kept
word for word in the page version below. Nothing in it conflicts with the
workstream rules. Two things are added for this set, and nothing is removed:

1. One sentence naming the course as educational, not treatment (decision D7).
2. A joint-specific paragraph pointing to the red-flag signs.

Not carried over: the "Have this reviewed before launch" box on the legal
page. It is an internal note, not disclaimer text. A10 owns any change to the
legal page itself; this file does not edit it.

The source course's closing line ("This content is educational only and does
not replace medical advice...") is replaced by the versions below.

## Page version

For the joint-load check (foot of the page and the results), the sales page,
the category page, each lesson screen, and each blog post's closing section.

> **Medical disclaimer**
>
> Everything on this site is educational. It does not provide medical advice,
> diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for evaluation by a
> qualified clinician.
>
> The assessments and calculators here estimate, they do not measure. Field
> tests carry meaningful error compared with laboratory testing, and normative
> bands describe populations rather than individuals. Treat any result as a
> starting point for a conversation with your own clinician.
>
> Talk with your physician or physical therapist before beginning a new
> exercise program — particularly if you have a heart condition, chest pain on
> exertion, uncontrolled blood pressure, a recent surgery, or have been
> inactive for some time. Stop any test or session for chest pain, dizziness,
> or unusual shortness of breath.
>
> Load as Medicine is an educational course, not treatment. After joint
> surgery, your surgeon's or physical therapist's protocol comes first.
> Stop and contact a clinician if a joint is hot, red or swollen with a fever,
> gives way or gets stuck, cannot take weight after a fall, or if your calf
> hurts or swells after a joint replacement.
>
> [Read the full medical disclaimer](/legal/medical-disclaimer/)

## Download version

Last page of every PDF, as its own block, not in small print. The link is
written out because a printed page cannot be clicked.

>
> This guide is educational. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or
> treatment, and it is not a substitute for evaluation by a qualified
> clinician. Talk with your physician or physical therapist before beginning a
> new exercise program, particularly after a recent surgery. After joint

>
> Full disclaimer: muscle-meta.com/legal/medical-disclaimer/

## Footer line

For page footers and any card or tool screen that already shows the page
version elsewhere on the same page.

> Educational, not medical advice. [Medical disclaimer](/legal/medical-disclaimer/)

## Rules

- The page or download version appears on every page and download. The footer
  line alone is not enough (`docs/legal-requirements.md`).
- Do not shorten the page version further without the clinical reviewer's
  approval. If a layout cannot hold it, use the footer line plus a link, and
  flag it to A9.
- Do not add reassurance such as "this is safe for everyone" next to the
  disclaimer.
