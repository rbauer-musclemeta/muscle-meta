How do you believe exercise and mechanical load impact a painful joint?

Muscle-Meta™ real-time reframe

Your joint is living tissue — not a rubber tire.

The single most damaging myth in joint health is that joints "wear and tear" from movement. Cartilage has no blood vessels of its own — it relies on the mechanical squeeze-and-release of joint movement to circulate nutrients in and waste out.

Complete rest doesn't protect your joint. It starves the cells that maintain it.
