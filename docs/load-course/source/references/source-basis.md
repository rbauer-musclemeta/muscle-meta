## Tier 4 — Research briefs and conversion drafts

Use these for audience fears, objections, lay translations, and topic-specific language. Do not automatically adopt their headlines, urgency, statistics, or claims:

- Endurance and Active Ager research briefs.
- Functional Independence research brief.
- Nutrition and Metabolic Flexibility research briefs.
- Recovery, Lifestyle, Stress Management, Balance, and Brain Health research briefs.
- Assessment and landing-page blueprints embedded in those files.

These sources contain valuable explicit “words to avoid” lists. They also contain some adversarial medical framing, fear amplification, causal overstatement, and time-window certainty. The guardrails in this skill intentionally refine those elements.

## External basis for CBT-informed language

- [Beck Institute: Understanding CBT](https://beckinstitute.org/about/understanding-cbt/) — collaboration, strengths and values, present orientation, guided discovery, action plans, skills, and progress monitoring.
- [Beck Institute: Cognitive Behavioral Wellness Coaching](https://cares.beckinstitute.org/wellness-coaching/) — nonclinical wellness-coaching applications and the boundary between coaching and treatment for diagnosed mental health conditions.
- [American Psychological Association: Understanding psychotherapy](https://www.apa.org/topics/psychotherapy/understanding) — psychotherapy as collaborative treatment delivered by qualified professionals.

## Interpretation rule

The skill captures the current workspace-derived Randy/Muscle-Meta voice. It is a strong first version, not a claim that every sample was personally written or individually approved by Randy. When Randy gives direct feedback, update the relevant rule and record whether it replaces or narrows a previous inference.
