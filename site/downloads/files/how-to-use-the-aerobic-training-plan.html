How to use your aerobic training plan

Seven short sections, one job each. Read it once, then come back to any section when you need it. The same guide is built into the plan file, under “How to use this plan”, so you only need one download.

About 5 minutes to readAlso built into the plan file
A

The plan in one minute

The plan runs 12 weeks. You build an easy aerobic base first, add a few harder efforts only when you are ready, and re-test in weeks 6 and 12 to see what changed.

Open the plan file in any browser on your phone, tablet or computer. Work through it top to bottom the first time:

  1. Safety check (step 1)
  2. Find your level (step 2)
  3. Effort zones (step 3)
  4. Your plan (step 4), then the Re-tests (step 5)
  5. Keep your progress (step 6)

After that, you will mostly live in step 4, Your plan.

B

Safety check and level

Safety check

Answer six yes or no questions about the past three months. The plan stays closed until you do.

  • Nothing flagged: you move straight on.
  • A clinician flag (chest pain, dizziness, a limiting condition, recent surgery): take the plan to your physician or physical therapist first. Tick the box once they have reviewed it, and the plan opens.
  • Wiped out for a day or more after ordinary effort: this progressive plan is not the right path yet. The page explains a gentler, pacing-first approach.

Choose your level

Three quick questions suggest a level. You can pick a different one, and you can change it later.

  • Gentle start: easy minutes only, no hard efforts, 35 to 70 minutes a week. Best if you are returning after a long break or exertion leaves you breathless.
  • Beginner: 3 sessions a week, one short interval session from week 5.
  • Advanced: 4 sessions a week, intervals from week 3, and a second interval day later in the plan. Best if you already train regularly.

When unsure, pick the lower level. Finishing a plan that fits beats struggling through one that does not.

C

Working through your weeks

Effort zones first

Enter your age to see your estimated maximum heart rate and zone table. If you take a beta blocker or another medicine that slows your heart rate, tick the box and ignore heart rate numbers. Use effort out of ten and the talk test instead.

  • Easy sessions: you can speak in full sentences. This is most of the plan, and it is meant to feel comfortable.
  • Hard intervals: short rounds where you can only say a few words. They are always followed by easy recovery.

Using the week view

  • The bar chart shows all 12 weeks. Tap a week to open it. Teal is easy minutes, orange is hard minutes.
  • Each week lists its sessions with the day suggested. Tap Show steps on a session to see the warm-up, the main work and the cool-down.
  • Tick a session when you finish it. The progress bar counts your ticks.
  • Set a First Monday date if you want weeks tied to the calendar. It is optional.
  • The days are suggestions. Moving a session by a day is fine. Doing two hard days back to back is not.

Intervals start only after a clear weekly check

In the first interval week, the plan tells you if your previous check was not clear. You can use the easy version of that week and start intervals the week after.

D

The weekly check

At the bottom of each week is a five-minute check, meant for Sunday. Answer four things: morning pulse compared with usual, energy out of ten, whether joints are sore, and whether your easy days stayed easy. Then press Check my week.

  • Green light: move on to next week as planned.
  • Hold steady: one warning sign. Repeat this week once, then check again.
  • Pull back: two or more signs. Use the easy version of next week, about a third less volume and no intervals, then resume.

Stepping back is part of the plan, not a failure. The notes box under each week is for anything you want to remember or tell your clinician.

E

Re-tests

Two simple measures show whether the plan is working: the six-minute walk distance, and your resting heart rate taken in bed the same morning.

  1. Do a baseline before week 1.
  2. Repeat in week 6 and week 12 (the plan marks those weeks).
  3. Use the same route or machine, the same time of day, and similar footwear.
  4. Walk the whole six minutes, no jogging. Stop for chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness.

Type your results into the Re-tests cards. Distances in yards are converted to meters, and the page shows the change against your baseline.

F

How to save your progress

There is no account and no sign-in. Your ticks, weekly checks, notes and test results are saved automatically in the browser you are using, on that device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The four buttons in step 6

  • Print or save as PDF: makes a clean one-page-per-week sheet for the fridge. In the print window, choose Save as PDF to keep a copy.
  • Download my progress: saves a small file to your device. Do this every few weeks as a backup, and before switching devices.
  • Load saved progress: pick that file to restore everything. Use it on a new phone, a new computer or after clearing your browser.
  • Start over: erases everything. It asks you to press twice, so it cannot happen by accident.

Know when your progress can disappear

Progress is tied to one browser on one device. It can be lost if you clear browsing data, use a private or incognito window, or open the plan in a different browser or on another device. Download your progress file regularly and you can always restore it.

Moving to another device

  1. On the old device, press Download my progress.
  2. Send the file to yourself by email or cloud storage.
  3. Open the plan on the new device and press Load saved progress.
G

Quick fixes

The plan will not open.
Finish the safety check and choose a level. If a clinician flag appears, tick the review box.
I missed a few days or a week.
Pick up at the same week, not the next one. After more than a week away, drop back one week.
I changed my mind about my level.
Choose another level card in step 2. Your plan view switches. Tick marks are saved per level.
My progress disappeared.
Check you are in the same browser and not a private window, then try Load saved progress with your latest download.
A session felt too hard.
Slow down, shorten the intervals or skip them, and do the weekly check. The easy version of a week is always available.
I have chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness.
Stop. Contact your physician before continuing.
