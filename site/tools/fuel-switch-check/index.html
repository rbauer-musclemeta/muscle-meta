The Fuel Switch Self-Check

Where is your dimmer set? In about three minutes, see how well your body switches between burning sugar and burning fat, and where to start turning it back up.

When that switch works, you can go a few hours without food and feel fine, walk before breakfast without feeling shaky, and recover overnight. When it sticks, your body leans on sugar, runs low fast, and recovery slows down. That is a skill your body can relearn at any age.

    Before you change anything

    This is an educational self-check, not a medical test or diagnosis. It can't measure your metabolism directly. Talk with your doctor before changing meal timing or exercise if you:

    • take medication for blood sugar (including insulin or GLP-1 medications) or blood pressure
    • are pregnant or breastfeeding
    • have a history of disordered eating
    • have a heart condition or have been told to limit exercise
    • have surgery scheduled

    Get checked if going without food often leaves you dizzy, faint, confused, or with a racing heart. Those aren't normal "crash" signs. See the medical disclaimer.

    Why this matters

    1. In 585 adults tested before major surgery, those whose bodies switched fuels best had fewer complications and a median hospital stay about 4 days shorter (9 vs 13 days). Promising

      Arina P, et al. Metabolic Flexibility as a Candidate Mechanism for the Development of Postoperative Morbidity. Anesth Analg. 2025. PMID 40175163. Observational, single centre.

    2. Adults in their 60s and 70s burned about 33% more fat during exercise after 16 weeks of endurance training. Promising

      Sial S, et al. Training-induced alterations in fat and carbohydrate metabolism during exercise in elderly subjects. Am J Physiol. 1998. PMID 9612234.

    3. People with metabolic syndrome burn less fat during exercise and shift to sugar earlier than fitter people. Established

      San-Millán I, Brooks GA. Assessment of Metabolic Flexibility by Means of Measuring Blood Lactate, Fat, and Carbohydrate Oxidation Responses to Exercise. Sports Med. 2018. PMID 28623613.

    4. The 12 questions and score bands are a teaching tool built from these everyday signs, not a validated clinical test. Unproven-Bridge

      Muscle-Meta Matrix clinical education, Randy Bauer, PT.
