Joint health risk assessment

A free, scored check for adults 40 and over. It looks at seven areas that shape how your joints hold up over time: past injuries, metabolic health, muscle, function, weight change, medications and current symptoms. You get a 0 to 100 Joint Risk Score, an action tier, your priority areas and a log you can use to track retests.

Before you start

What this assessment asks, and where your answers go

It takes about 12 minutes, including a 30-second chair test. It is an educational tool. It does not diagnose arthritis or any other condition, and the score is a guide to action, not a prediction.

Your privacy

What we ask:

  • Seven safety questions about warning signs that need a clinician first.
  • Your age and sex, past joint injuries, height, weight and a few health conditions.
  • Medications that can affect joints, tendons, muscle or bone, and your current joint symptoms.

Where your answers go: your score is worked out in this browser. Nothing is sent to us while you answer. At the end you choose what to keep:

  • Save on this device. Keeps the date, your score, tier, the seven area scores and any flag codes in this browser so you can compare retests. It does not keep your answers. You can delete any entry, or all of them, at any time.
  • Save to my account (when you are signed in). Sends your answers, including medication and health-condition answers, to your Muscle-Meta account so your results are kept with your other assessments. Only you and staff supporting your account can see them.
  • Neither. Close the page and nothing is kept. You can still print the summary.

Area D of 7

Fitness and function

Simple tests show how your joints and muscles work together under everyday demand.

The 30-second chair stand

  1. Use a sturdy, standard-height chair with its back against a wall, and a counter within reach.
  2. Sit in the middle of the seat, feet flat, arms crossed over your chest.
  3. For 30 seconds, stand up fully and sit back down as many times as you can. Count each full stand.
  4. Stop if pain goes above 5 out of 10 or you feel unsteady.
Please enter your chair-stand count (or tick the box) and answer every question.

    About this assessment

    This assessment is for education only. It does not diagnose, treat or predict any medical condition, and it is not a substitute for an evaluation by a physician, physical therapist or other qualified healthcare professional. Do not start, stop or change any medication based on your results.

    The point values are educational weightings informed by research, not a validated clinical prediction model. They rank relative importance; they do not calculate your personal chance of developing arthritis. Version 1.0.0, pilot.
